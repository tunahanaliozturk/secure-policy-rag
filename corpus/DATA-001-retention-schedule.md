# Data Retention Schedule

## 1. Principle
Personal data is kept only as long as a legal duty or a documented business need requires it. When the retention period ends, the data is deleted or irreversibly anonymised within 90 days.

## 2. Customer due diligence records
Identity documents and due diligence records are kept for five years after the customer relationship ends, as anti-money laundering law requires.

## 3. Transactions and accounting
Transaction records and accounting documents are kept for seven years after the end of the financial year they belong to, to meet tax law.

## 4. Recorded calls and chats
Recorded telephone calls and electronic communications of investment services staff are kept for five years, and up to seven years when the regulator asks for it.

## 5. CCTV and applicants
CCTV footage of branches is kept for four weeks unless it is needed for an incident. Data of rejected job applicants is deleted four weeks after the procedure closes, or kept for one year with the applicant's consent.
