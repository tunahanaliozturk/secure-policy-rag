# Customer Due Diligence Procedure

## 1. Identification
Before an account is opened, the customer's identity is verified against a valid passport, national identity card or residence permit. Copies of identity documents are stored in the customer file, never on local drives or in email.

## 2. Risk rating
Every customer receives a money laundering risk rating of low, medium or high when the relationship starts. The rating considers the customer's country, occupation, expected transactions and products.

## 3. Periodic review
High-risk customers are reviewed every year, medium-risk customers every three years and low-risk customers every five years. A review that is more than 90 days overdue blocks new products for that customer.
