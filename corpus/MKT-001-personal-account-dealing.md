# Personal Account Dealing

## 1. Who is covered
This policy applies to all employees with access to inside information or client orders, and to trades made for their partner or children under 18.

## 2. Pre-clearance
Every personal trade in shares, bonds or derivatives must be pre-cleared by Compliance in the personal dealing tool. A pre-clearance is valid until the end of the next business day.

## 3. Holding period
Instruments bought on a personal account must be held for at least 30 calendar days. Losses are not a reason to sell earlier.

## 4. Prohibited trades
Employees may not trade instruments on the restricted list, may not trade ahead of client orders, and may not trade in spread bets or contracts for difference.
