# Unusual Transaction Reporting and Tipping-Off

Control code: CC-AML-002-P5VJ

## 1. Reporting duty
Once an investigator concludes that a transaction is unusual, the Money Laundering Reporting Officer files a report with the Financial Intelligence Unit without delay, and no later than two business days after the conclusion.

## 2. Tipping-off prohibition
No employee may tell a customer, or anyone outside the investigation team, that a report has been filed or is being considered. Breaching this rule is a criminal offence and leads to dismissal.

## 3. Open investigations
Investigation Kestrel concerns a network of 14 accounts used to move funds from online fraud. Investigation Ember concerns trade-based laundering through a textile importer. Both are under a hold instruction: accounts may not be closed without approval from the Reporting Officer.

## 4. Record keeping
Reports and the supporting analysis are kept for five years after the report date in the restricted investigations archive.
