# Cyber Incident Response Runbook

Control code: CC-SEC-004-X3QA

## 1. Severity levels
Severity 1 is any incident affecting payment systems, customer funds or more than 10,000 customer records. Severity 1 incidents are escalated to the CISO within 15 minutes and to the Management Board within one hour.

## 2. Crown jewel systems
The crown jewel systems are the payments hub, the core banking ledger, the online banking platform and the identity provider. Network isolation of a crown jewel system requires approval from the CISO or the deputy CISO.

## 3. Regulatory reporting
A major ICT-related incident is reported to the supervisor under DORA with an initial notification within four hours of classification and no later than 24 hours after detection.

## 4. Out-of-band communication
During a severity 1 incident the response team uses the out-of-band bridge, not corporate email or chat, because those may be compromised.
