# Personal Data Breach Response

## 1. Report immediately
Any employee who suspects a personal data breach reports it to the Privacy Office within 24 hours, through the incident form on the intranet at https://intranet.bank.example/privacy/breach. Do not try to investigate the breach yourself.

## 2. Notification to the authority
If the breach is likely to put people's rights at risk, the Data Protection Officer notifies the data protection authority within 72 hours of the Bank becoming aware of it.

## 3. Informing customers
If the risk to customers is high, they are informed without undue delay, in plain language, with advice on how to protect themselves.

## 4. Breach register
Every breach is entered in the breach register, including breaches that did not need to be notified, with the facts, the effects and the remedial action taken.
