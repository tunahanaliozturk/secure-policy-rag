# Disciplinary Procedure

Control code: CC-HR-004-B1SC

## 1. Investigation first
Before any disciplinary measure, HR and the line manager investigate the facts. The employee is told what they are accused of and may respond in writing within five business days.

## 2. Measures
Possible measures are a written warning, a final written warning, suspension with pay during the investigation, and dismissal. Dismissal for an urgent reason requires approval from the HR director and the Legal department.

## 3. Representation
The employee may be accompanied by a colleague or a union representative at every hearing.

## 4. Records
A written warning is removed from the personnel file after two years if there has been no further incident.
