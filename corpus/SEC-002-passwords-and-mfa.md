# Password and Multi-Factor Authentication Standard

## 1. Password length
Passwords for personal accounts must be at least 14 characters long. Passphrases made of several words are encouraged. Passwords are not changed on a fixed schedule, only when there is a sign of compromise.

## 2. Multi-factor authentication
Multi-factor authentication is required for remote access, for email on mobile devices, and for every administrative account. SMS codes are not accepted as a second factor for administrative accounts; use the authenticator app or a hardware key.

## 3. Lockout
An account is locked for 15 minutes after 10 failed sign-in attempts.

## 4. Password managers
Employees must use the Bank's approved password manager for any password they cannot remember. Writing passwords down is not allowed.
