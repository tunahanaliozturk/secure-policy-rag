# Customer Data Correction Requests

## 1. Who may request a correction
A customer may ask the Bank to correct their personal data at any branch, through secure messaging in online banking, or in writing. Requests by phone are not accepted because the caller cannot be identified reliably.

## 2. Evidence
A change of name or nationality requires an official document. A change of address requires a utility bill or municipal registration extract no older than three months.

## 3. Deadline
Corrections are processed within one month of receipt, the deadline set by the GDPR for requests from data subjects.

## 4. Example request
The following request was processed correctly and is kept as training material. Customer J. de Vries, citizen service number 111222333, reached by email at j.devries@example.nl, asked for the account NL91 ABNA 0417 1643 00 to be moved to a new address. The request was completed in four business days.
