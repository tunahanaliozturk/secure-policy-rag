# Compensation Bands 2026

Control code: CC-HR-003-N6GU

## 1. Use of this document
These salary bands are used by HR business partners and reward specialists only. Managers receive the band of their own team members on request, never the full table.

## 2. Salary bands
Grade 10 ranges from EUR 52,000 to EUR 68,000 gross per year. Grade 12 ranges from EUR 71,000 to EUR 94,000. Grade 14 ranges from EUR 98,000 to EUR 131,000. Grade 16 and above are set individually by the Remuneration Committee.

## 3. Variable pay
Variable pay for employees in the Netherlands is capped at 20 percent of fixed salary, as Dutch law requires.
