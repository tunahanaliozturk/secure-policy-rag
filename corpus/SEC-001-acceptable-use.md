# Acceptable Use of IT Resources

## 1. Business use
Bank laptops, phones and accounts are for business use. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not interfere with work and does not break any other rule in this policy.

## 2. Storage and transfer
Bank information may only be stored on Bank systems. Personal cloud storage, personal email and USB storage devices may not be used for Bank information.

## 3. Phishing
Report suspicious emails with the Report Phishing button in the mail client, or through https://intranet.bank.example/security/report. Never forward a suspicious email to colleagues.

## 4. Monitoring
The Bank monitors the use of its systems to protect customers and the Bank, within the limits set by law and the works council agreement.
