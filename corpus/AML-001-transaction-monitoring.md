# Transaction Monitoring Alert Handling

Control code: CC-AML-001-R7WB

## 1. Alert triage
Alerts from the transaction monitoring system are triaged by a level 1 analyst within five business days. The analyst either closes the alert with a documented reason or escalates it to level 2.

## 2. Level 2 investigation
A level 2 investigator completes the investigation within 20 business days of escalation and decides whether the activity is unusual and must be reported to the Financial Intelligence Unit.

## 3. Quality assurance
Each month a quality assurance team re-checks a random sample of 5 percent of closed alerts. An analyst whose error rate exceeds 10 percent in a month receives targeted retraining.

## 4. Example alert
Alert 2026-0441 concerned card 4111 1111 1111 1111, used for 37 cash withdrawals in four countries within two days. It was escalated to level 2 and reported.
