# Credit Watchlist and Forbearance

Control code: CC-CR-004-M2PL

## 1. Watchlist triggers
A business client is placed on the credit watchlist when a payment is more than 30 days past due, when a financial covenant is breached, or when the internal rating drops by three or more notches within twelve months.

## 2. Watchlist meetings
The watchlist committee meets every two weeks. Each watchlist client has an action plan with an owner and a deadline, reviewed at every meeting.

## 3. Forbearance measures
Forbearance means a concession to a client in financial difficulty, such as a payment holiday or an extended term. A forborne exposure keeps that status for at least two years and must be flagged in the core banking system on the day the concession is granted.

## 4. Transfer to restructuring
Clients who are more than 90 days past due, or unlikely to pay in full without the sale of collateral, are transferred to the Financial Restructuring and Recovery team within ten business days.
