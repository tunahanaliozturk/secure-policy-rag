# Enhanced Due Diligence for Politically Exposed Persons

## 1. Who counts as a PEP
A politically exposed person is someone who holds or held a prominent public function, such as a head of state, minister, member of parliament, senior judge or board member of a state-owned company. Family members and known close associates are treated the same way.

## 2. Approval
Opening or continuing a relationship with a PEP requires written approval from a member of senior management, in addition to the normal account opening checks.

## 3. Source of wealth
For every PEP the Bank establishes both the source of wealth and the source of funds, with documentary evidence such as tax returns, sale contracts or audited accounts.

## 4. Ongoing monitoring
PEP relationships are always rated high risk and reviewed every year. A person stays a PEP for at least 12 months after leaving the public function.
