# Privileged Access Management

Control code: CC-SEC-003-L9FE

## 1. Just-in-time access
Administrators do not hold standing privileged rights. Privileged access is requested in the access vault for a specific change ticket and expires after at most eight hours.

## 2. Session recording
All privileged sessions on production systems are recorded. Recordings are kept for one year and reviewed by the security operations centre on a sample basis.

## 3. Break-glass accounts
Break-glass accounts are used only when the access vault is unavailable. Each use is reported to the CISO within one hour and the password is rotated afterwards.

## 4. Quarterly recertification
Line managers recertify every privileged account in their team each quarter. Accounts not recertified within 14 days are disabled.
