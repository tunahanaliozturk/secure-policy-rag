# Corporate Lending Limits

Control code: CC-CR-002-7QX4

## 1. Approval authority
Credit decisions for business clients follow the approval matrix below. Exposure is measured per group of connected clients, not per legal entity.

## 2. Approval matrix
Branch credit committees may approve total group exposure up to EUR 2 million. Regional credit committees approve exposure between EUR 2 million and EUR 25 million. The Group Credit Committee approves anything above EUR 25 million, and any exposure to a client rated 8 or worse on the internal scale, whatever the amount.

## 3. Four-eyes principle
Every approval needs a relationship manager signature and an independent credit risk signature. The same person may never sign in both roles for one client.

## 4. Annual review
All business credit facilities are reviewed at least once a year. A facility that misses its review date by more than 60 days is frozen for new drawings until the review is completed.
