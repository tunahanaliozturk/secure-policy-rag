# Sanctions Screening

## 1. Lists
Customers, beneficial owners and payment counterparties are screened against the sanctions lists of the European Union, the United Nations, the Dutch government and the United States Office of Foreign Assets Control.

## 2. Payment screening
Every outgoing and incoming international payment is screened before it is executed. A payment that produces a possible match is held until a sanctions analyst clears or confirms it.

## 3. Clearing hits
A possible match may only be cleared as a false positive by two analysts working independently, the four-eyes principle. A confirmed match is frozen and reported to the regulator on the same day.

## 4. Customer base rescreening
The full customer base is rescreened every night against the latest version of each list.
