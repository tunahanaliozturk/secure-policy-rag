# Information Classification Standard

## 1. Four levels
All information is classified at one of four levels: public, internal, confidential or restricted. The owner of the information sets the level and reviews it at least every two years.

## 2. Public and internal
Public information may be shared with anyone. Internal information may be shared with all employees and contractors under contract, but not outside the Bank.

## 3. Confidential
Confidential information is limited to the owning department and to people who need it for their work. It must be encrypted when sent outside the Bank's network.

## 4. Restricted
Restricted information is limited to named people. Disclosure could cause serious harm to customers or the Bank. Restricted documents may not be printed, and access is logged.
