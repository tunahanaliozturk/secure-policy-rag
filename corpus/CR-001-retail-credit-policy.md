# Retail Credit Policy

## 1. Scope
This policy covers consumer loans, credit cards and residential mortgages offered to private individuals. Business lending is covered by the Corporate Lending Limits standard.

## 2. Affordability
The total monthly debt service of an applicant, including the new loan, may not exceed 40 percent of verified net monthly income. Income is verified with the two most recent payslips or, for self-employed applicants, the last two annual tax returns.

## 3. Mortgage loan-to-value
A residential mortgage may not exceed 90 percent of the market value of the property. For buy-to-let properties the maximum loan-to-value is 70 percent. The property value comes from an independent valuation report no older than six months.

## 4. Minimum age and term
Applicants must be at least 18 years old. The loan term may not run past the applicant's 75th birthday for consumer loans, and the mortgage term is capped at 30 years.

## 5. Declined applications
A declined applicant receives the main reason for the decision in writing within five business days. Staff may not suggest ways to adjust an application so that it passes the affordability test.
