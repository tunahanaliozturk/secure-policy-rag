# Hybrid and Remote Work Policy

## 1. Hybrid pattern
Employees in office roles may work from home up to three days a week, agreed with their manager. Teams agree at least one shared office day each week.

## 2. Working from abroad
Working temporarily from another country is allowed for at most 20 working days per calendar year, and only from countries in the European Economic Area, because of tax and data protection rules.

## 3. Home workplace
The Bank pays a one-off home office allowance of EUR 500 and a monthly internet allowance of EUR 25. Employees must work in a place where screens cannot be seen by others.

## 4. Exceptions
Customer-facing branch roles, trading desk roles and data centre roles are excluded from the hybrid pattern.
