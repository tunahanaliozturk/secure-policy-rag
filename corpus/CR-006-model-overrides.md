# Credit Rating Model Overrides

Control code: CC-CR-006-H4TN

## 1. When an override is allowed
An analyst may override the rating produced by the credit rating model only on the basis of information the model does not use, such as a pending merger or a court ruling. The reason must be documented in the rating tool.

## 2. Limits
An override may move a rating by at most two notches. Larger overrides need approval from the head of credit risk.

## 3. Monitoring
Model Validation monitors the override rate every quarter. If more than 10 percent of ratings in a portfolio are overridden, the model owner must review whether the model is still fit for purpose.
