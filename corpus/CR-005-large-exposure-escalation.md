# Large Exposure Escalation Register

Control code: CC-CR-005-Z8KD

## 1. Purpose
This register lists client groups whose exposure exceeds 10 percent of the Bank's eligible capital, and the escalation decided for each. It is shared with the Management Board and the Chief Risk Officer only.

## 2. Current escalations
Project Heron: a shipping group with total exposure of EUR 412 million. Exposure is to be reduced to EUR 300 million by the end of the second quarter through a syndication. Project Lantern: a real estate developer with total exposure of EUR 286 million, placed under enhanced monitoring with monthly reporting to the Board.

## 3. Regulatory limit
No client group may exceed 25 percent of eligible capital. Any expected breach is reported to the Chief Risk Officer the same day.
