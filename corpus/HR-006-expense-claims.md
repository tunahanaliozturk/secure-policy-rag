# Expense Claims Guide

## 1. Submitting a claim
Expense claims are submitted in the expense tool within 60 days of the expense, with a photo of the receipt. Claims older than 60 days are not reimbursed.

## 2. Meals and hospitality
Business meals with clients are reimbursed up to EUR 75 per person. Gifts and hospitality above EUR 100 must be registered in the gifts register before they are accepted or given.

## 4. Approval
Claims are approved by the line manager. Claims by managers are approved by their own manager, never by a team member.
