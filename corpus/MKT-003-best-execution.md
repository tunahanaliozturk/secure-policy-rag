# Order Execution Policy Summary

## 1. Best execution
When the Bank executes client orders in financial instruments, it takes all sufficient steps to obtain the best possible result. Price and costs are the most important factors for retail clients.

## 2. Execution venues
The Bank executes orders on regulated markets, multilateral trading facilities and with selected market makers. The list of venues is reviewed every year.

## 3. Client instructions
If a client gives a specific instruction, for example to trade on one venue, the Bank follows that instruction, which may prevent it from obtaining the best result.

## 4. Annual report
Each year the Bank publishes the top five execution venues per class of instrument.
