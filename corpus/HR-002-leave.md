# Leave Policy

## 1. Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per year, plus public holidays. Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to their contract hours.

## 2. Carrying over leave
At most five unused days may be carried over to the next year. Carried-over days expire on 1 July of that year.

## 3. Parental leave
The Bank pays 70 percent of salary during the first nine weeks of statutory parental leave. Partners receive five days of paid birth leave, taken within four weeks of the birth.

## 4. Mandatory block leave
Employees in trading and payment roles must take at least ten consecutive business days of leave each year, during which they have no access to their systems. This is a fraud control, not a wellbeing measure.
