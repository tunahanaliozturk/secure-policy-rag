# Restricted and Watch List Procedure

Control code: CC-MKT-004-Y2HR

## 1. Purpose
The restricted list holds issuers on which the Bank has inside information. The watch list is known only to Compliance and is used to monitor trading without alerting the desk.

## 2. Current restricted names
Northwind Logistics, in connection with a planned public offering advised by the Bank. Halvorsen Energy, in connection with a confidential refinancing. Trading in these names for own account and personal accounts is prohibited.

## 3. Adding and removing names
Only the Compliance control room adds or removes names. A name is removed no earlier than two business days after the information has become public.
