# Speak Up and Whistleblowing Policy

## 1. What to report
Report suspected wrongdoing such as fraud, bribery, breaches of law, or serious risks to customers. You do not need proof, only a reasonable suspicion.

## 2. How to report
Report to your manager, to Compliance, or anonymously through the Speak Up line at https://intranet.bank.example/speakup, which is run by an independent provider.

## 3. Protection
The Bank does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith. Retaliation is a disciplinary offence.

## 4. Follow-up
The reporter receives an acknowledgement within seven days and feedback on the follow-up within three months.
