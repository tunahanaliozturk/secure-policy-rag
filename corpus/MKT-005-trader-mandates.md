# Trader Mandate Limits

Control code: CC-MKT-005-D8JW

## 1. Mandates
Every trader has a written mandate naming the products they may trade, their position limits and their loss limits. Trading outside the mandate is a limit breach.

## 2. Value at risk
The one-day 99 percent value at risk of the rates desk may not exceed EUR 4 million. The FX desk limit is EUR 2.5 million.

## 3. Stop-loss
A trader who loses more than EUR 500,000 in a calendar month must stop opening new positions until the head of the desk has reviewed the losses.

## 4. Breach reporting
Market Risk reports every limit breach to the head of the desk and to the Chief Risk Officer by the next business day.
