# Collateral Valuation Procedure

## 1. Independent valuers
Only valuers on the approved panel may value collateral. A valuer may not value a property for a client they are related to or have worked for in the past two years.

## 2. Revaluation frequency
Residential property held as collateral is revalued at least every three years using an indexed valuation, and with a full valuation when the loan is restructured. Commercial real estate is revalued every year with a full valuation.

## 3. Haircuts
Listed shares pledged as collateral receive a haircut of 40 percent. Government bonds from euro area issuers rated AA or better receive a haircut of 5 percent. Cash collateral in the loan currency receives no haircut.

## 4. Insurance
Pledged buildings must be insured against fire for at least their rebuild value, with the Bank named as loss payee on the policy.
