# Trading Desk Communication Rules

## 1. Recorded channels only
Conversations about client orders or trades may only take place on recorded channels: desk phones, Bank mobile phones with recording enabled, and the approved chat platform.

## 2. Banned channels
WhatsApp, personal text messages, personal email and other unrecorded apps may never be used for business communication, even to say that a call is needed.

## 3. Retention
Recordings are kept for five years, in line with the Data Retention Schedule.

## 4. Breaches
Using an unrecorded channel for business is a disciplinary matter. The first breach leads at least to a written warning.
